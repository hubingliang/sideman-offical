Support

Questions about Sideman, subscriptions, Apple Music playback, or something that looks wrong in the guide.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Get in touch

Email brianhu.cn@gmail.com with subject “Sideman Support.” Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots help when credits or bios look off.

Subscriptions and billing

Paid features are billed through the App Store (via Apple and RevenueCat). Sideman does not take card details or run its own account system. Free includes catalog search and browsing with a daily limit on newly opened detail pages; Pro removes that quota. Credits and As Sideman lists stay readable on Free.

To manage, renew, or cancel a subscription, use Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device, or the subscription tools in the App Store / in-app Customer Center. For refunds or charge disputes, contact Apple Support — only Apple can reverse App Store purchases.

Privacy and your data

Sideman works without its own account. Preferences, favorites, browse-quota counters, listening stats, and notification settings stay on your device. If you enable iCloud sync in the app, favorites write to your Apple iCloud private database. There is no Sideman account deletion flow because there is no Sideman account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Apple Music

In-app playback uses Apple MusicKit after you authorize Apple Music and have a valid subscription. Availability depends on Apple. Listening stats inside Sideman count only in-app playback and stay on your device.

Credits and content

Session credits, dates, roles, and short bios come from third-party catalogs and editorial curation. They may be incomplete or wrong. If you spot an error worth fixing, email the details (album, session, or musician) — corrections help the next App Store update.

Album covers and portraits may load from remote hosts. Reference links (for example Wikipedia or Discogs) open outside Sideman.

About the native update

Sideman is now a native SwiftUI app. The interactive relationship graph from earlier builds is no longer part of the product; browse via Today, Explore search, and entity details instead. Home-screen widgets and optional Apple Music playback are available in current releases.

Feedback

Feature ideas and usability notes are welcome at the same address. Mention whether you are writing about the free experience or Pro so we can prioritize.
