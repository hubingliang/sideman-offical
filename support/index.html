Support

Questions about Sideman, subscriptions, or something that looks wrong in the guide.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Get in touch

Email brianhu.cn@gmail.com with subject “Sideman Support.” Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots help when credits or bios look off.

Subscriptions and billing

Paid features are billed through the App Store (via Apple and RevenueCat). Sideman does not take card details or run its own account system. To manage, renew, or cancel a subscription, use Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device, or the subscription tools in the App Store.

For refunds or charge disputes, contact Apple Support — only Apple can reverse App Store purchases.

Privacy and your data

Sideman works without an account. Preferences (theme and optional developer flags) stay on your device; clearing app data removes them. There is no account deletion flow because there is no account. See the Privacy Policy for how network requests and third-party links work.

Credits and content

Session credits, dates, roles, and short bios come from third-party catalogs and editorial curation. They may be incomplete or wrong. If you spot an error worth fixing, email the details (album, session, or musician) — corrections help the next update.

Album covers and portraits may load from remote hosts. Listening and reference links open Spotify, Wikipedia, or other services outside Sideman.

Feedback

Feature ideas and usability notes are welcome at the same address. Mention whether you are writing about the free experience or a paid feature so we can prioritize.
