Privacy Policy

Sideman is designed to work without an account.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

What we collect

We do not require sign-in. We do not run an analytics SDK in the current build. Preferences you choose in Settings (appearance, language, and developer preview flags) stay on your device.

On-device storage

Theme, language, and optional developer Pro preview are saved locally with on-device storage so they survive app restarts. Clearing app data removes them. Sideman does not operate a user account system, so there is no account deletion flow.

Network activity

Album covers and musician portraits may load from remote hosts (for example Spotify, Discogs, or Wikimedia). Opening Spotify, Wikipedia, Apple Music, or other links leaves the app and follows that service’s privacy policy.

Third parties

Sideman does not sell personal data. When you follow an external link, that destination may process data under its own terms. Future subscription billing (for example RevenueCat via the App Store) will add purchase metadata handled by Apple and the billing provider—not a Sideman account system. If we add analytics, crash reporting, accounts, or cloud sync later, this policy will be updated before those features ship.

Children

Sideman is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact
