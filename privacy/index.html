Privacy Policy

Sideman works without an account.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

What we collect

No sign-in required. This build does not include an analytics SDK. Preferences you set (appearance and optional developer flags) stay on your device. Language follows the system App Language setting.

On-device storage

Theme and optional developer Pro preview are saved on this device so they survive restarts. Clearing app data removes them. Sideman does not operate a user account system, so there is no account deletion flow.

Network activity

Album covers and musician portraits may load from remote hosts (for example Spotify, Discogs, or Wikimedia). Release builds may contact Expo’s update service for corrected app or graph data. Opening Spotify, Wikipedia, or other links leaves the app and follows that service’s privacy policy.

Third parties

Sideman does not sell personal data. External links are governed by the destination’s terms. Subscription billing (RevenueCat / App Store) is handled by Apple and the billing provider — not a Sideman account system. This notice will be updated if we add accounts, analytics, or cloud sync.

Children

Sideman is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
