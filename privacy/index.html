Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Sideman, a native iOS app. Sideman does not require its own account.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Information we collect

Sideman does not collect a name, email address, phone number, or similar profile information through the app, and does not run its own user-account system. This version does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Preferences, favorites, browse-quota counters, listening stats, and notification settings stay on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, favorites are written to your Apple iCloud private database; we cannot read that private data.

Information stored on your device

Your device may store theme and language preferences, recent Explore searches, favorited musicians / albums / labels and track favorites, daily free detail-browse quota counters, local listening stats from in-app MusicKit playback, and the On This Day local-notification preference. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes on-device information. When iCloud sync is enabled, favorites also sync to your iCloud private database and remain subject to your Apple ID and iCloud settings.

Apple Music

If you authorize Apple Music, Sideman may use Apple MusicKit to match and play available content in the app. Playback, subscription status, and media-library access are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Sideman keeps aggregated listening-time stats from in-app playback on your device only and does not upload listening history to our servers.

Notifications

You may enable On This Day local notifications. They are scheduled on your device from catalog content and do not go through a Sideman push server. You can turn them off at any time in system Settings or in the app.

Network requests

The app may download covers and portraits from third-party hosts (for example Apple, Discogs, or Wikimedia). Favorites sync communicates with Apple CloudKit; in-app purchases communicate with the App Store and RevenueCat; authorized playback communicates with Apple Music. The jazz catalog is primarily a local database bundled with the app and updated with App Store releases. If you open an external link (for example Wikipedia or Discogs), that service’s privacy policy applies.

Third-party services

We do not sell personal information. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple and RevenueCat under their respective terms and privacy policies. iCloud / CloudKit and MusicKit / Apple Music are provided by Apple. Opening links to other apps or websites is subject to those parties’ policies.

Children

Sideman is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
